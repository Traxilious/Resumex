## Slide 1: Title & Introduction
- **Title**: ResumeX - Smart Resume Scoring & ATS Feedback Platform
- **Sub-heading**: Optimizing Job Applications with NLP & Automated ATS Evaluation

## Slide 2: Problem Statement
- Over 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS filters before reaching human recruiters.
- Candidates lack clarity on keyword optimization, missing skills, and formatting standards.

## Slide 3: Proposed Solution - ResumeX
- Web-based platform delivering instant resume scores (0–100), ATS keyword alignment, authenticity %, Pros/Cons breakdown, and learning roadmaps.

## Slide 4: Key Modules & Architecture
- **Multi-Engine Document Parser**: PDF, DOCX, TXT + RapidOCR for scanned image PDFs.
- **ATS Keyword Engine**: 21+ pre-seeded role skill profiles + custom field input.
- **Scoring & Authenticity Checker**: 5-parameter weighted scoring algorithm & pattern detector.

## Slide 5: Live Demonstration
- Demo of uploading a resume, target role selection, score gauge animations, Chart.js breakdown, and portfolio project suggestions.

## Slide 6: Conclusion & Impact
- Empowers candidates with actionable guidance to bypass recruiter screening filters.
